NESTPOLY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

NESTPOLY is a mobile puzzle game developed and published by Mono Craft Studio, based in Argentina.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use NESTPOLY, including information processed through third-party advertising services.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

NESTPOLY stores certain information locally on your device to operate the game and remember your preferences. This may include:

  • Game progress and saved game states
  • Scores and gameplay records
  • Sound, language, and game settings
  • Advertising-related state and preferences

NESTPOLY does not require account registration or login. The game does not operate its own server for uploading or synchronizing player progress.

Some locally stored data may be included in Android device backup and restore processes, depending on the device and operating system settings.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

NESTPOLY uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK and participating advertising partners may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and advertising identifiers, where available
  • Advertising interactions and performance information
  • Application and device diagnostic information

This information may be used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and other advertising-related purposes, subject to applicable laws and privacy choices.

More information is available in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads privacy documentation .

3. Advertising Privacy Choices

Where applicable, NESTPOLY uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present advertising-related privacy choices and manage consent information.

Depending on your region and applicable requirements, you may be able to consent to, decline, or manage certain advertising data processing activities.

Your Android device may also provide controls relating to advertising identifiers and privacy preferences.

For questions about advertising-related privacy choices, please contact us using the email address listed below.

4. Gameplay Analytics

NESTPOLY does not integrate a separate gameplay analytics service such as Firebase Analytics or GameAnalytics.

This does not prevent Google AdMob and its partners from processing advertising-related measurement and diagnostic information.

5. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the game or its advertising service providers may be used to:

  • Operate the game and preserve local progress
  • Remember player preferences
  • Display and manage advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Prevent advertising fraud and address technical issues
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

6. Data Sharing and Security

Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and participating advertising partners in connection with the advertising services provided within NESTPOLY.

The game does not operate its own system for selling player scores or saved gameplay progress to third parties.

Game progress is stored using local application storage. Google describes encryption in transit and other security practices for its advertising SDK.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Age Requirements and Children

NESTPOLY is intended for users aged 13 and older. Children under the age of 13 are not part of the game's intended audience.

NESTPOLY does not require users to enter their date of birth or verify their age to play.

The game does not intentionally request names, email addresses, or account registration details from children under 13.

Privacy and advertising requirements for minors may vary by country. In some jurisdictions, the applicable age for consent to certain data processing activities is higher than 13.

Users under 18 are encouraged to review privacy information with a parent or legal guardian.

If you believe that information relating to a child has been processed inappropriately, please contact us.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally saved game information generally remains on the device until it is removed through available application or device controls, or the application is uninstalled.

Android backup and restore features may affect whether local data is retained or restored.

Google and its advertising partners process and retain advertising-related information under their applicable privacy and retention policies.

You may contact Mono Craft Studio for privacy questions or requests involving information under our control.

9. International Data Processing

Advertising service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence, in accordance with their applicable policies and legal obligations.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in NESTPOLY, third-party services, or applicable requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
